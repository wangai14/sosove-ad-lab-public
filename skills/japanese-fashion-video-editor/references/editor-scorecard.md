# Japanese Womenswear Editing Scorecard

Use the correct scorecard for the task. Do not use a material-pool score as a finished-ad score.

## Material-Pool Readiness (100 points)

### Role coverage: 45

Required visual roles:

- `hook`: 1+ strong opening candidate.
- `try_on`: 2+ wearable front/side/back/full-body moments.
- `detail`: 1+ fabric or construction close-up.
- `motion`: 1+ walking, turning, drape, or daily-action moment.
- `proof`: 1+ shot that visibly supports a selling point.
- `ending`: 1+ clean full-body or CTA-ready moment.

Optional roles: `transition`, lifestyle scenes, store/social proof, packaging, and offer stills.

### Candidate quality: 20

Reward high smart scores, clear framing, usable duration, and moments that remain understandable after a short trim. Resolution alone earns no points.

### Visual diversity: 15

Reward different sources, angles, scales, locations, and actions. Penalize more than 45% of candidates sharing one role or repeated moments from the same source range.

### Edit readiness: 10

Reward candidates with frame analysis, cut hints, source ranges, or other evidence that supports deterministic trimming.

### Quantity: 10

- 8+ usable candidates: supports a 15-30 second cut.
- 5-7: normally supports a focused 15-second cut.
- Fewer than 5: not enough for reliable automatic editing.

## Finished-Ad Readiness (100 points)

### Hook: 20

- Product is visible immediately.
- The first meaningful visual change occurs within roughly 1.5 seconds.
- The opening promise is understandable without sound.

### Product clarity: 10

Show silhouette, garment length, fit, and at least one useful viewing angle at mobile size.

### Proof and copy alignment: 15

Every major benefit claim has matching visual evidence. Full-body copy stays on full-body footage; detail copy stays on detail footage.

### Pacing and shot variety: 15

For cold-traffic Meta/TikTok ads, use roughly 1.2-2.2 second visual states for most of the cut. Longer holds are acceptable only when an action or important message develops.

Reward source-contiguous blocks that extract multiple distinct moments from one original video before switching models or locations. Shot variety does not require a different source on every cut. Penalize unnecessary `A -> B -> A` source alternation, abrupt model/location ping-pong, and repeated use of the same visual state.

### Offer communication: 15

Price, discount, scarcity, trial, or guarantee must be visually scannable. Narration-only offers are weak. Use one clear hierarchy rather than several competing badges.

### CTA and ending: 10

End with a clean product impression, brand/product identity, and one action such as `今すぐチェック`.

### Captions and audio: 10

Use phone-readable Japanese typography, safe margins, two lines or fewer when practical, and no collision with source text. Keep narration intelligible over music and effects.

### Compliance: 5

Avoid body shaming, unsupported numerical transformation claims, misleading before/after framing, and unverifiable stock, deadline, price, or popularity claims.

## Score Bands

- `90-100`: Excellent. Ready for final QA and variant production.
- `75-89`: Good. Usable for launch after targeted fixes.
- `60-74`: Usable. Structure works but important conversion elements are weak.
- `40-59`: Weak. Suitable only for a rough test or internal review.
- `<40`: Not ready. Rebuild the structure or repair the material pool.

## Diagnosis Rules

- If required roles exist but the timeline uses them poorly, report an **editing gap**, not a material shortage.
- If a claim lacks visual evidence across the entire pool, report a **material gap** and give a specific reshoot action.
- If a direct-response offer lacks an offer shot, recommend a motion-graphic or product-still card before requesting a reshoot.
- If the first three seconds are weak, fix the hook before adding transitions, color grading, or music.
- If a 30-second cut is repetitive but the pool is adequate, recommend a 15-25 second version rather than calling the pool insufficient.
- If individual clips are strong but the cut feels jumpy, regroup the same-source moments contiguously before requesting new footage or adding transitions.

## Copy Alignment

| Copy type | Matching visual evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Silhouette / styling | Full-body, try-on, motion, ending |
| Fabric / construction | Detail, hand interaction, macro texture |
| Comfort / coverage / drape | Proof, try-on, motion |
| Commute / date / daily use | Matching lifestyle or styling scenes |
| Popularity / store proof | Real store, customer interaction, checkout, social proof |
| Price / discount / trial | Offer card, readable product still, CTA-ready visual |

## Production-Language Examples

- `P0 0.0-3.0s：首镜头停留过长。保留商品全身和路人询问两个动作，各 1.2-1.5 秒，并加“銀座で10人以上に聞かれた”标题。`
- `P1 14.5-18.6s：面料文案匹配，但两个特写构图重复。保留垂坠动作，替换一段为手揉或透气细节。`
- `P0 25.3-32.6s：优惠只存在于旁白和字幕。增加 11,880円划线、6,880円主价格和 2点目15%OFF 的专用价格卡。`
- `P1 0.0-8.0s：镜头按 A-B-A-C 来回切换，人物和场景不连续。将 A 素材中的走路、被询问、多人围观连续成组，再进入下一条产品展示素材。`
- `素材足够做一条15-25秒广告；若要扩展到三条变体，需要补拍正侧背三视角和面料互动镜头。`
